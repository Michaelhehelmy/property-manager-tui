How to use Reservations
  • Create: fill guest, dates, room, rate and submit “Create reservation”. Availability and pricing come from real data.
  • Open a reservation: click its ID to view folio, add payments/POS, book activities/trips (added to folio & invoice), check-in/out, modify, cancel/no-show, or generate invoice.
  • Search/filter: use the search box to find by guest, room, or status.
  • Protected actions: some actions (refunds/voids, paid cancel/no-show, checkout override) may require unlock/super password as configured.
